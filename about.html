About EasyFix Agent

We help regular people use AI tools with confidence — no jargon, no hype, no sponsored nonsense.

Founder

Muhammad Mudassir Yaseen — Founder & Editor-in-Chief of EasyFix Agent. Passionate about making AI accessible to everyone through honest, hands-on testing and plain-language guides.

What we do

EasyFix Agent started as a simple idea: most AI coverage is either too technical or too salesy. We test free AI tools ourselves — the chatbots, image generators, video makers, voice tools and productivity apps — and write plain-language guides about what actually works.

How we test

  • We use the free tier of every tool, because that is what most readers will use.
  • We run real tasks — write an article, make a thumbnail, edit a short video — not demo prompts.
  • We update guides when free plans change, which happens often.

How we make money

This site is supported by advertising. We clearly label ads, and our reviews are never for sale — a tool cannot pay its way into a recommendation.
