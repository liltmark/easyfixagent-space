The EasyFix Services Studio

Guides teach you how. The Studio does it for you — presentations designed, documents formatted, transcripts typed — by our small home-based team, with every delivery checked twice before it reaches you.

What the studio does

  • Presentation design — investor pitch decks, business presentations and thesis-defense slides, designed from your content. Real charts, one consistent style, editable files. From $35.
  • Thesis & document formatting + proofreading — APA 7, MLA, Harvard, Chicago/Turabian, IEEE or your university's own template: headings, citations, references, Table of Contents, title page. From $35 per 5,000 words.
  • Transcription & captions — Urdu and English audio/video transcription, and subtitles for Shorts and creator videos. From $10 per order.
  • Social media content & management — Canva post packs and monthly management packages for small businesses. Monthly packages — ask for a quote.
  • Marketplace listing writing — Amazon and eBay listing copy written to be found and read. Per listing — ask for a quote.

Prices above are starting points for standard jobs. Message us with your document, deadline and requirements and we will give you an exact quote before you pay anything.

How ordering works

  • 1. Brief — email us your document, notes or draft, plus your deadline and any template you must follow.
  • 2. We build — a member of our studio team does the work using our checklists and templates for that service.
  • 3. Quality check — nothing is delivered until it passes our internal QC pass: structure, accuracy, formatting compliance.
  • 4. Delivery — you receive editable files (PPTX, DOCX, PDF) and a fair revision window to ask for adjustments.

You can also order the two launch services — presentation design and thesis formatting & proofreading — through our Fiverr profile, where the same packages and prices apply.

Our promise to students — read this first

We format, edit, proofread and design slides from work you have written. We do not write theses, dissertations, essays or assignments, we do not sit exams, and we never invent or alter data, results or references. That line protects your degree and our studio — and it does not move.

Work with us — join the studio team

Our work is done by home-based workers in Pakistan, and we especially encourage women working from home to apply. We are building a small, vetted team for the services above. If you have one of these skills, we would like to hear from you:

  • PowerPoint / presentation design (Canva-only designers also welcome for social posts)
  • Careful English editing and proofreading; APA/MLA formatting
  • Fast, accurate typing and Urdu/English transcription
  • Amazon/eBay listing writing

The deal, stated honestly:

  • Joining starts with a small paid test task so both sides can judge the fit fairly.
  • You are paid 50–70% of the net amount we receive for each job you complete and we approve — the share is agreed with you per service before you start.
  • Payment is weekly, by JazzCash or Easypaisa.
  • Work depends on the orders clients place. This is per-job freelance work — there is no fixed salary and no guaranteed income, and anyone who promises you otherwise is not being straight with you.
  • You work from home, on agreed deadlines. Your name stays behind the studio brand; clients deal with us, we deal with you.

To apply, email info@easyfixagent.space with the subject "Studio Application": tell us your skill, your city, the hours you can give, and attach one sample of your own work (a deck you designed, a document you formatted, or a transcript you typed).
