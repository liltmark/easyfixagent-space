8 AI Tools Every Small Business Should Use (Free Plans)

October 18, 2026 · Business · 6 min read

Hiring is expensive. These AI tools let a small business punch far above its weight — all with free plans to start.

1. ChatGPT / Claude — your copywriter

Service descriptions, social posts, email replies, ad copy. Draft in seconds, edit in minutes.

2. Canva — your designer

Logos, flyers, social graphics with AI-assisted layouts. The free tier covers most small-business needs.

3. Tidio (free tier) — your receptionist

AI chatbot for your website that answers common questions 24/7 and captures leads while you sleep.

4. CapCut — your video editor

Auto-captions and templates turn phone footage into professional shorts for social media. Free.

5. Wave — your bookkeeper

Free accounting software with AI categorization. Invoicing and receipts without the spreadsheet pain.

6. Mailchimp (free tier) — your marketer

Email campaigns with AI subject-line help. The free plan covers up to 500 contacts — enough to start.

7. Google Business Profile — your storefront

Not AI, but essential: free listing on Google Maps and Search. AI-assisted post suggestions included.

8. Perplexity — your researcher

Competitor research, pricing checks, market questions — answered with sources in seconds.

Where to start

Pick the bottleneck: no website traffic? Start with content tools. Missing calls? Start with the chatbot. One tool, one month, measure the difference — then add the next.

Honest note: AI tools multiply effort; they don't replace it. The businesses winning with AI are the ones that also show up consistently.
