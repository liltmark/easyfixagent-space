How to Make AI Videos for Free: A Beginner's Step-by-Step Guide

October 20, 2026 · Guides · 7 min read

You do not need a camera, editing software, or a budget to make a real video anymore. You need one good sentence. This guide walks you through the exact workflow we use every day — from a plain idea to a finished, downloadable clip.

Step 1: Start with one scene, not a whole movie

Every AI video tool works in short clips — usually 2 to 10 seconds. Professionals don't fight this; they build videos scene by scene, the way films are actually shot. Decide your first scene only: who is in it, where are they, and what happens.

Step 2: Write the prompt with the 4-part formula

The difference between a blurry mess and a cinematic clip is almost always the prompt. Use this formula:

  • Subject: who or what — "a plumber in blue overalls and a red cap"
  • Action: what moves — "tightening a pipe as water sprays, then the leak stops"
  • Setting: where — "a bright modern bathroom, morning light"
  • Style words: "photorealistic CGI, cinematic, ultra-detailed"

That is it. "A plumber fixing a leaking tap in a kitchen" already works — the formula simply makes it work better.

Step 3: Generate in a free studio

Open our free AI Video Studio, paste your prompt, and generate. The studio first paints a cinematic frame, then films it, and finishes in HD. You get 3 free videos a day — enough to build a real short, scene by scene, across a few days.

Step 4: Add a voice (optional, powerful)

A 20-second script read by an AI voice turns a pretty clip into a story. Keep sentences short, write the way people talk, and let the voice carry the lesson while the video carries the drama.

Step 5: Stitch scenes and finish

Join your clips in order, put the strongest moment first (viewers decide in 2 seconds), add quiet background music under any voice, and export in the format your platform wants: vertical 1080×1920 for Shorts and Reels, horizontal 1920×1080 for regular posts.

Three mistakes that waste free generations

  • Asking for everything at once — one clip, one action. Crowded prompts produce chaos.
  • Forgetting the style words — without them you may get a flat cartoon when you wanted cinema.
  • Judging frame one — generation is a numbers game. Pros regenerate; the keepers are worth it.
The honest truth: free AI video rewards patience, not money. Three good scenes a day beats thirty rushed ones — your channel grows on the keepers.
