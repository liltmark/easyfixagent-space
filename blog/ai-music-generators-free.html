5 Free AI Music Generators: Make Songs Without Instruments

October 18, 2026 · AI Tools · 6 min read

Need background music for a video but can't compose? These five free AI music generators turn a text description into a finished track in minutes.

1. Suno — the full-song machine

Type "upbeat lo-fi hip hop about rainy days" and get a complete song with vocals. The free tier gives you daily credits — enough for several tracks a week. Best overall quality in 2026, and the results genuinely surprise people.

2. Udio — the producer's playground

Similar idea, slightly more control over structure: intros, verses, remixing sections. The free plan is generous for experimenting. Great when Suno's style doesn't fit and you want to steer the arrangement.

3. Beatoven.ai — royalty-free background music

Built specifically for creators: pick a mood, length and genre, get background music you can actually use in monetized videos. The free tier covers short projects. If YouTube copyright claims scare you, start here.

4. Soundraw — the customizer

Generate a track, then tweak energy, instruments and length with sliders instead of re-prompting. The free trial lets you test the workflow. Ideal for podcast intros where you need exactly 15 seconds of a specific vibe.

5. Stable Audio — the open option

From the Stable Diffusion team: text-to-audio with an open model behind it. The free web tier produces solid short clips and sound effects. Less polished than Suno for full songs, but great for stingers, loops and ambience.

Copyright: the fine print that matters

Free does not always mean yours to sell. Suno and Udio grant you rights to creations on paid plans; free-tier tracks usually allow personal and social use but check the current terms before using them commercially. Beatoven is the safest bet for monetized content.

How to get good results

Be specific about genre, mood, tempo and instruments — "dark synthwave, 100 BPM, driving bass, no vocals" beats "cool music" every time. Generate three versions, keep the best 30 seconds, and loop it. Nobody will know it is AI.
