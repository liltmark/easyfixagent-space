AI Video Generator No Sign Up: 7 Free Tools Tested

November 1, 2026 · Guides · 6 min read
Written & tested by Muhammad Mudassir YaseenFounder, EasyFix Agent — every guide on this site is tested by hand before it is published.
🗓 Last updated: November 2026

Search for a "no sign up" AI video generator and every tool promises exactly that — until you press generate and meet an email form. So we tested seven of them the way a real visitor would, and noted the exact moment each one asks for something: an account, an email, or your card. Only the first tool on this list truly asks for nothing at all.

Quick answer

If you want to make an AI video right now with literally no account, use our free AI Video Studio: it runs entirely in your browser on free public engines, with no sign-up and no watermark added by us. Every other tool below is free to test, but each one puts its sign-up wall somewhere — we tell you exactly where, so there are no surprises.

1. EasyFix Agent Video Studio — genuinely no sign-up

Our own studio, and the reason this site exists. Type a scene in plain words (text-to-video) or upload a photo to animate (image-to-video), and the request goes straight from your browser to a free public AI engine — LTX-Video, two Wan 2.2 engines and FLUX for starting images. No account, no email, no card, and nothing is stored on a server of ours because this site does not have one. The honest limits: the free engines are shared with the whole internet, so they queue jobs and cap anonymous use — at busy times generation pauses for a while, then reopens. If you want an allowance nobody else can drain, the studio lets you paste your own free Hugging Face token; it stays in your browser only and simply unlocks a larger, private quota on the same engines. Clips are short (two seconds succeeds most reliably), so plan to stitch a few together for longer pieces.

2. Magic Hour — no account to look, credits to play

Magic Hour lets you open its tools and try the workflow with a small free allowance before any commitment, which is more generous than most. The catch comes in two parts: free usage is measured in credits that run out fast when you experiment, and free-plan exports carry a watermark. It is a good place to test whether AI video suits your project — just do not plan a whole channel around the free tier.

3. Hedra — talking photos, email at the door

Hedra specialises in making a still portrait talk and sing, and it is genuinely impressive at it. The sign-up reality: you need an account to generate, and the free tier works out at roughly 300 credits a month with watermarked output. If your goal is a talking photo rather than general video, that free allowance is still useful — our separate guide to short-form social videos shows how clips like these fit into a posting workflow.

4. Vidu — free trial credits after sign-up

Vidu's image-to-video quality is smooth, and new accounts receive free trial credits without a card. Those credits are a tasting menu, not a plan: a handful of clips, at capped resolution, and then the paywall. Treat Vidu as a free audition — if its style is exactly what your project needs, you will know within the trial; if not, you have spent nothing.

5. Viggle — character motion, app-style flow

Viggle makes a character from your photo copy the movement of a reference video — the engine behind a million dancing-meme clips. It works through an app and Discord-style flow rather than a simple web page, the free tier is restricted and watermarked, and output is playful rather than polished. For memes it is the right tool; for anything else on this page, the others serve you better.

6. Hailuo (MiniMax) — beautiful results, one-time trial

Hailuo produces some of the most cinematic free-tier footage available, which is why its limits sting: the free allowance is essentially one-time trial credits rather than a monthly refill, exports are watermarked, and resolution is capped at 768p on the free route. Use your trial credits on your single most important shot, not on experiments.

7. Runway — the pro option's free sample

Runway is the tool professionals reach for, and its free plan is a one-time bundle of credits — enough to render a few short clips and feel the quality difference, not enough for regular publishing. An account is required and the credits do not refill. Our full Runway AI review breaks down what those credits actually buy and when upgrading makes sense; for a steady free workflow, our broader round-up of free AI video generators compares the refillable options.

How the seven compare

  • Truly no sign-up at all: only the EasyFix Agent Video Studio (tool 1).
  • Account needed, free credits refill: Hedra (monthly, watermarked).
  • Account needed, one-time trial credits: Vidu, Hailuo, Runway.
  • Watermark on free exports: Magic Hour, Hedra, Viggle, Hailuo — but not our studio.
  • Best single free shot quality: Hailuo or Runway trial credits, spent carefully.
  • Best everyday free workflow: the studio, stitched clips, patience at busy times.

FAQ

Why do "no sign up" tools still ask for my email? Because generation is expensive. The free, anonymous allowance is a shared demo pool; an email account is how a tool gives you a private quota and stops abuse. The honest difference between tools is where the wall stands — before your first clip, or after several.

What is the Hugging Face token option in your studio? A free key from Hugging Face that you paste into the studio page. It never reaches us — your browser sends it directly to the engine — and it swaps the shared anonymous allowance for your own, larger one. Still free, still no account with us.

Can I remove the watermarks from the other tools free? Not legitimately. Watermark removal is the paid upgrade on those platforms. If a clean export matters, generate in the studio instead, where nothing is added.

Which tool should a complete beginner start with? The studio: open the page, type one sentence, get a clip. Our step-by-step beginner's guide to making AI videos free walks from that first clip to a finished, stitched video.

The verdict

"No sign up" is mostly marketing. One tool on this list means it literally — the browser studio here — and the rest are free auditions with the wall in different places. Now you know where each wall stands: pick the audition that fits your project, and keep the studio for everyday free clips.
