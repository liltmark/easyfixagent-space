About the author

Muhammad Mudassir Yaseen is the founder of EasyFix Agent. He started the site because most AI coverage is either too technical or too salesy. He is passionate about making AI accessible to everyone through honest, hands-on testing and plain-language guides.

How he tests

  • He uses the free tier of every tool, because that is what most readers will use.
  • He runs real tasks — write an article, make a thumbnail, edit a short video — not demo prompts.
  • He updates guides when free plans change, which happens often.
