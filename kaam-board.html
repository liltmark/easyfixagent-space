Kaam Board — Real Work, Honest Pay

This board is for people in Pakistan — especially those working from home — who want real work, not promises. Below are three honest ways work reaches you through EasyFix: our own studio's client orders, two partner platforms you can join directly, and simple guides for the easiest jobs. Read the rules at the bottom before you start anything.

Studio Orders — hamaray apne clients

These are real orders that clients place with the EasyFix Services Studio. The work is done by our home-based team, checked by us, and delivered under the studio's name. The services clients order from us:

  • Data entry & product listings — spreadsheets, copy-paste work, store listing uploads
  • Presentation design — pitch decks and slides designed from client content
  • SEO content — articles and on-page work for small-business websites
  • Video services — editing, captions, thumbnails and channel work

Your share: you are paid 80% of the net amount we receive for each job you complete and we approve. Payment is weekly, sent by us directly to your own JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank account — the account number you give us is used only for your payout, and is never shared with anyone else.

How to join:

  • 1. Read the work guide — the beginner guide further down this page.
  • 2. Do a paid test task — a small real task, paid, so both sides can judge the fit fairly.
  • 3. Get approved work — pass the test and studio orders start coming to you as clients place them.

Work depends on the orders clients place — this is per-job freelance work, with no fixed salary and no guaranteed income. Anyone who promises you otherwise is not being straight with you.

Partner Platforms — seedha wahan kaam

Note: kaam un ke platform par hota hai — hum sirf raasta aur rehnuma dete hain. Kisi platform ke tasks yahan copy nahi kiye jate, aur hum un platforms ki taraf se koi wada nahi karte. (The work happens on their platform — we only show the way and guide you. No platform's tasks are copied here.)

SproutGigs

A micro-task platform open to workers in Pakistan: small data entry jobs, transcription, sign-ups, categorisation and social tasks, mostly paying a few cents each. With steady mehnat, a realistic expectation is roughly Rs. 140–560 per day — it depends entirely on how many tasks are available and how many you finish; that is an honest range, not a promise. Payouts via PayPal, Skrill, Airtm or crypto, from a $5 minimum.

Microworkers

Another long-running micro-task platform that accepts Pakistani workers: transcription, data extraction, categorisation and other small tasks. The same honest expectation applies — roughly Rs. 140–560 per day with steady work, depending on task supply. Payouts via Airtm, PayPal, Skrill or Payoneer, with minimums of about $9–$20 depending on the method.

Asan Kaam ki Rehnuma — the 5 easiest jobs

  • Transcription (sun kar likhna) — pehle free AI se draft nikalo, phir audio sun sun kar ghaltiyan theek karo; sirf tawajjo chahiye, koi degree nahi.
  • Captions / subtitles — CapCut mein auto-captions chalao, ghalat alfaaz theek karo, har line 5–7 alfaaz se zyada na ho.
  • Canva posts — tayyar template kholo, sirf matn aur tasveer badlo; brand ke rang aur font kabhi apni marzi se na badlo.
  • Data entry / listing upload — aik waqt mein aik item: naam, phir qeemat, phir tafseel, phir tasveer — aur qeemat hamesha do bar check karo.
  • Document safai / formatting — checklist dekh kar headings, hawalay (references) aur safha numbers theek karo; jo samajh na aye use highlight karke chor do — andaza kabhi nahi lagana.

Board ke Usool — rules that protect you

  • Koi fees nahi — joining and getting work here is free. Jo bhi tum se pehle paisay mange, wo hum nahi hain.
  • Koi jhoota wada nahi — earnings depend on real work and real orders; koi fixed income ki guarantee nahi di jati.
  • Account number sirf payout ke liye — tumhara JazzCash/Easypaisa/bank number sirf tumhe paisay bhejne ke liye istemal hota hai.
  • VPN se doosre mulk ke offers karna mana hai — using a VPN to do another country's tasks or offers gets accounts banned and earnings seized, on every platform. Kabhi mat karna.
